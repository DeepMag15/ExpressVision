# Ready-to-send message to Express Pesticides

Copy, adjust the names, send. **Keep it short** — the full specification is the
attachment, and a long email is one that gets read later and then never.

The single most important thing is that it asks for **two quick items first**.
Both take them minutes, both unblock our planning, and neither requires anyone to
go and export hundreds of gigabytes. Asking for everything at once is how these
requests stall.

---

## Subject

> ExpressVision — two quick things to get started, and one bigger one

---

## Body

> Hi [Name],
>
> We've finished building the detection system and it's running end to end. To
> make it work on your clients' sites, we now need to teach it what pests look
> like **on those specific cameras** — and for that we need some real footage.
>
> There's no shortcut here: there's no existing collection of night-time CCTV
> footage of rodents in Indian warehouses, so the system has to learn from yours.
>
> **Two quick things first** — both should take only a few minutes, and they let
> us start planning straight away:
>
> **1. A list of cameras** at two or three sites you'd like to pilot. Just the
> names or locations as they appear in the NVR is enough — for example "Dock 01",
> "Dry store aisle 3". We'll then tell you which handful are worth exporting,
> rather than you exporting everything.
>
> **2. Written confirmation** that you're able to share this footage with us for
> developing the system, and that the site owner is aware. Overnight recordings
> will contain staff, so we want this on record before anything is sent.
>
> **Then the bigger one:** once we've agreed which cameras, we'd like **3–5
> consecutive nights of recordings, roughly 22:00 to 06:00**, from those cameras.
>
> Two things that matter a lot here:
>
> - It needs to be the **main (primary) stream**, not the sub-stream. The
>   sub-stream is much lower quality, and a rat in it is about a dozen pixels
>   across — genuinely unrecoverable, by any software.
> - It's large: roughly **10–20 GB per camera per night**. That's too big to
>   email, so a portable hard drive is usually the practical route. We're happy
>   to supply one or come and collect it.
>
> I've attached the full details, including a short form for the camera
> information (lens, mounting height, distance to the floor) — but please don't
> let that hold up items 1 and 2.
>
> One thing worth flagging honestly: part of what this first batch tells us is
> **which existing cameras can actually see a pest and which cannot**. Some
> almost certainly won't — most CCTV is aimed at people at head height, not at
> rodents on the floor. It's far better for both of us to find that out now than
> six months in.
>
> Happy to jump on a call if that's easier than email.
>
> Best,
> [Your name]

## If there's no reply in a week

Chase **only the camera list**. It's the smallest possible ask, it costs them
almost nothing, and it lets us do real work — we can tell them which cameras to
export and start estimating coverage. A follow-up asking for everything again is
easy to defer; a follow-up asking for one list is easy to answer.
