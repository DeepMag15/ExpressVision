1. Which industries will use it and what problems are you trying to solve?
Industries:
•	Food processing and manufacturing 
•	Warehouses and logistics 
•	Hotels and restaurants 
•	Hospitals 
•	Residential and commercial buildings 
•	Factories 
•	Agriculture 
•	Waste management facilities 
Problems to solve:
•	Early detection of pest activity 
•	Difficulty in continuously monitoring large premises 
•	Manual CCTV monitoring is time-consuming 
•	Identifying pest movement and entry points 
•	Detecting repeated pest activity 
•	Providing photographic/video evidence 
•	Improving pest-control inspection and IPM decisions 

2. Which objects should be detected?
The first stage should detect:
•	Rats and mice 
•	Dogs and cats 
•	Birds 
•	Lizards/geckos 
•	Snakes 
•	⁠Flying Insects
•	Other relevant animals/Insects

3. What analytics should be generated?
The system should generate:
•	Number of pest detections 
•	Detection frequency 
•	Time and date of activity 
•	Peak activity time 
•	Movement direction 
•	Movement path 
•	Duration of activity 
•	Repeated visits 
•	High-activity locations 
•	Camera-wise pest activity 
•	Pest species distribution  (OPTIONAL)
•	Pest activity heat map  (OPTIONAL)
•	Comparison of activity over days/weeks/months 

4. What are the expected final outputs?
The final system should provide:
Live Output
•	CCTV feed with AI detection box 
•	Pest/animal identification 
•	Confidence score 
•	Movement tracking 
Alert Output
•	Real-time pest detection alerts 
•	Repeated activity alerts 
•	High-activity alerts 
Evidence
•	Screenshot 
•	Video clip 
•	Date/time 
•	Camera/location 
•	Movement path 
Report Output
•	Daily/weekly/monthly reports 
•	Pest activity trends 
•	Heat maps  (OPTIONAL)
•	High-risk areas
•	Recommendations for inspection

5. Do they require IoT sensors or just analysis of movements of objects and tracking?
Use of IOT sensors is a must, in all shades of projects but limited to areas where it can be used or may be most needed. 
e.g. Use of IoT for temperature monitoring as part of data collection to correlate the ideal temperature when pest movement is recorded.
